How the trial flow works — and how accounts stay clean

A plain-English walkthrough of what happens when a parent books a trial, what it means for you, and the safeguards that keep MindBody free of duplicate accounts.

The flow, start to finish

1
Parent books online
Picks a club, class, and time (up to 7 days ahead; cancelled classes never show), and enters their own details + the child’s name and date of birth.
2
Accounts are created & linked — automatically
The app makes the parent’s MindBody profile (their real email) and the child’s (a private placeholder email), and links the child to the parent with a Parent/Guardian relationship. If the parent already exists, it reuses them — never a second copy.
3
Staff get an email
An “ACTION NEEDED” email lands with the parent, child, and class, and three buttons: Confirm / Deny / Reassign (valid 3 days).
4
Confirm books it
Confirm enrolls the child in the class in MindBody, moves the HubSpot deal to Scheduled Trial, and the parent gets a confirmation. Done.

What it means for you

For the admin
Ibtissam / marketing
•The parent↔child family link you asked for happens automatically at booking — no manual linking.
•Every booking flows into HubSpot: a deal named “Child — parent email”, tied by a correlation ID to the MindBody booking, feeding your existing form, reporting, and workflows.
•The duplicate-deal issue is fixed, and the staff-notification + approval workflows are live.
•Open/optional decisions you own: parent self-service login, and rollout to the other clubs.
For the front desk
Staff
•One job: Confirm / Deny / Reassign each trial from the email.
•Don’t create accounts in MindBody — the booking already did, linked and clean.
•No merging in the normal flow. (One rare exception below.)
•If a Confirm fails (class cancelled/full), use Reassign or call the parent — the page tells you why.
For the new parent
Website visitor
•Books a trial online in about two minutes.
•Their MindBody profile and their child’s are created and linked automatically — nothing for them to set up.
•Staff confirms; they get a confirmation email and just show up.
•They don’t need a MindBody login to do a trial — staff manages it. A self-service login is an optional add-on later.

How duplicate accounts are avoided

Three safeguards run automatically:

✓Existing parents are reused, not recreated. The app checks the parent’s email first, so a returning parent never spawns a second account. (A second child for the same family is a new, correct record — not a duplicate.)
✓The child uses a private placeholder email, not the parent’s. Two accounts sharing one email is exactly what MindBody reads as a duplicate — so we don’t do that. The child is tied to the parent by the Parent/Guardian relationship instead.
✓Let the app create the accounts. If someone also hand-creates the same parent or child in MindBody, that makes a duplicate — so leave account creation to the booking.
⚠ The one way a duplicate can appear
If a parent sets up their own one-login “family account” in MindBody and re-adds their child, that creates a second child profile. It’s rare (most parents never log in). If it happens, merge the two — keep the family-member profile (merge, don’t delete, so the trial history carries over). Steps are in the staff guide.
Bottom line: a booking creates a linked parent + child automatically, with no duplicates. Staff just approve. A duplicate only appears if a parent self-builds a family account — and that’s a quick one-time merge.
