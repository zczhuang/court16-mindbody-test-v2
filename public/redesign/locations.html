Pricing, transparently

Why pricing varies by club.

FiDi and Brooklyn run higher (Manhattan real estate, premium hours). Ridge Hill, Fishtown, and Newton run lower. Members get the same unlimited access at every club — pricing differences only apply to drop-in classes.
