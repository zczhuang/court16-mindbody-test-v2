Family-account booking flow — validation

Live test on NY — Ridge Hill production · booked July 9, backend-verified July 10, 2026 · shared-email design confirmed with MindBody's Family Accounts team (case 05463499, Jul 7)

What changed

The online kids-trial flow now puts the parent's real email on the child's MindBody record (no more kid+…invalid placeholder) and links the two records with MindBody's built-in Parent/Guardian ↔ Child relationship at creation time. This is the design MindBody's Family Accounts team validated: with a shared email, the parent's account claim covers both records ("Both can be claimed"), and every child-level communication or mailing-list export carries a real, reachable address.
