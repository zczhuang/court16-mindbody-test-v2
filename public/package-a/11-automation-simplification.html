One message, one owner: simplifying the trial automation

What runs today across Mindbody's seven sites and the HubSpot portal, where the same job is done twice, and the exact change list to remove the redundancy before multi-club launch.

Evidence

Headline
Every redundancy traces to one root cause: two systems (and previously two intake paths) each believing they own the same customer message or the same record. The fix is the single-owner map — not more automation.
