Template 3 · Parent Mindbody account nudge

Revised draft in HubSpot · workflow off

Trigger & workflow

  • Workflow: Spec 06.
  • Candidate enrollment: Deal enters Trials pipeline in Requested Trial; enrollment alone is not permission to send.
  • Rule: Mindbody sends the secure password/claim link; HubSpot only reminds after a trusted parent_claim_pending check. Workflow 1820551993 remains off until that branch exists and is tested.
To: Parent
Subject: Finish setting up your Court 16 / Mindbody account
Tell the parent that Mindbody sent a separate secure Welcome email; ask them to use its password link and complete verification. Say that the child needs no separate login and that an already-finished parent may ignore the reminder. Do not include a Court 16 password button, a generic Forgot Password link, or a copied Mindbody token. Exact copy and live-test screenshots: Spec 06 visual handoff.
