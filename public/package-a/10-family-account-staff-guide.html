Complete a parent + child family account

A screenshot-based decision guide for club staff. Start with the parent record, finish on the original child record, and use the ready-to-send messages at each stop.

1

Check the parent record

Search the parent by email and open the parent client record. Read the link shown under Mindbody account.

If it says Connect Mindbody Account: the parent has not claimed the account. Do not click it for them. Send the claim nudge below.
Mindbody email to parent

Ridge Hill test shown. The parent must use the secure create/change-password link in Mindbody's own welcome email. The email template wording may change; the secure Mindbody link is what matters.

Ready to send · Claim nudge

Hi [Parent first name] — Court 16 created your Mindbody account for [Club] when you requested [Child]’s trial. Please look for an email from @mindbodyonline.com with “Welcome” in the subject (check spam/promotions), click the create/change password link, and complete Mindbody’s verification. [Child] does not need a separate login. Reply DONE when finished and we’ll connect [Child] to your family.

If it says Manage Family Account: the parent has claimed the account. Continue to Step 2.
2

Have the parent add the existing child to Family

The parent signs in to Mindbody Account Management, then goes to Account Info → Family → Add Member.

Parent sees child under Family

This screenshot shows the result after Add Member. It does not yet prove the original Court 16 child record is linked; staff still verifies that record in Steps 3–4.

Ready to send · Add Member

Thanks — now sign in at https://account.mindbodyonline.com/manageAccount. Under Account Info → Family, choose Add Member and add [Child full name] exactly as entered on the trial request (and DOB if prompted). Use Add Member only inside your family account; do not register [Child] again or create a separate child login. Reply ADDED when [Child] appears under Family.

3

Open the original child record and connect once

Return to the correct club, open the original child client ID created by the trial request, and look under Mindbody account.

Original child · Click once

If the original child says Connect Mindbody Account, click that link exactly once. Mindbody then sends the secure link; staff must not copy the link into HubSpot or send it manually.

  • Confirm the client ID matches the trial request before clicking.
  • Click Connect Mindbody Account one time.
  • Tell the parent to select the existing child in Mindbody's secure flow.
Ready to send · Link nudge

Thanks — we sent one final secure Mindbody email to [email]. Open the message with “Add Court 16” or “Link your account” in the subject and select the existing family member [Child]. Please do not add another child. Reply LINKED when complete.

Relationships are a pre-check, not completion

Not the success gate

Parent/Guardian and Pays For relationships are useful and should point to the intended child. They do not prove the child is visible and manageable in the parent's consumer account.

4

Verify the original child record is complete

Refresh and reopen the same original child client ID. The only success state is Manage Family Account.

Complete
Completion gate passed The original child shows Manage Family Account, the parent is the account owner, and the child appears under Family Members.
Ready to send · Complete

You’re all set. [Child] is linked to your Mindbody family account, so you can manage [Child] from your parent login.

Internal log note

Family account linked | Club: [Club] | Parent: [Parent full name] | Child: [Child full name] | Child MB ID: [original child client ID] | Manage Family Account verified: [timestamp] | Staff: [name]

Stop and escalate — do not improvise

  • The parent or child is in the wrong Court 16 site.
  • The account owner is not the intended parent.
  • The child record you found does not match the original client ID on the trial request.
  • A duplicate child appears, or Mindbody asks you to create a second child.
  • The parent replied LINKED, but the original child still says Connect Mindbody Account after refresh.
Never create, merge, move, or delete records to get past an error. Never give a child a password, create a separate child login, or paste a secure Mindbody link into HubSpot. Escalate with club, parent email, parent client ID, original child client ID, the exact state shown, and a screenshot.
