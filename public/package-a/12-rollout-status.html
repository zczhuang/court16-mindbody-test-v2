1 · What's built

The booking product DEPLOYED · BOOKING CLOSED

  • Kids-trial flow — club picker → live Mindbody calendar → family form → staff-confirmed booking, replacing the 12-field form + 11-step account email. Booking remains restricted to a verified dedicated trial Program; regular kids schedules are a separate read-only preview.
  • Court 16 design language — Gilroy type, brand yellow, doodle hero, and an exact replica of the court16.com footer — built to drop into court16.com/request-free-trial once launch checks and design sign-off land.
  • All-site calendar preview — parents can open every club today. Public regular-kids Programs are shown for planning only, Ridge Hill remains filtered to trial Program 61, and booking stays closed until launch checks pass.
  • Family accounts done right — one shared parent email, the site's verified Parent/Guardian link, no duplicate child profiles, no merges.

The safety architecture BUILT · DARK

  • Deal booking ledger — every request is one HubSpot Deal with a unique key; one unresolved request per parent; staff links are signed and single-purpose.
  • Double-write protection — write-ahead markers plus exact Mindbody read-backs are designed so a retry cannot charge or enroll twice; proven in offline contract tests, with the live end-to-end run still ahead. Failures land in a documented reconciliation runbook.
  • Duplicate-family protection — recorded Mindbody IDs are recovered from past request history (even denied ones) and reused instead of re-created; a live repeat-family fixture still has to prove it in production.
  • Fail-closed gates — a club cannot appear publicly, and real Mindbody writes cannot happen, until per-club evidence and server-side switches say so.

The documents WRITTEN · IN REVIEW

  • Staff family-account guide — four-state, screenshot-based SOP with the real Mindbody emails parents receive and exact HubSpot logging.
  • Automation simplification audit — the seven same-job-done-twice redundancies and the single-owner fix list.
  • Email & family-link decision doc — the option-1 contract; Ibtissam's copy/classification review and stop-branch tests are still open.
  • In-repo: multi-site readiness audit, owner access handoff (with the six activation links generated 17 Jul), HubSpot property/workflow contracts, reconciliation runbook. Old merge-based June docs carry superseded banners.
