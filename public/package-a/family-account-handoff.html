Family account handoff

Two short documents: the proposed email and automation changes, followed by the staff procedure for completing and verifying a parent-child family account.

Current decision · 18 Jul 2026
Use the parent's email on both original Mindbody records. Create exactly one parent and one child, and write the site-validated Parent/Guardian relationship.
Secure messages
Mindbody alone sends password, verification, and child-link tokens. HubSpot may explain or remind. Trusted nudge workflow 1853127851 now has the pending-state re-check and remains off; all other Cedarwind workflows are also off.
Completion
Reuse the original child Client ID—never recreate the child. The family is complete only when that child shows Manage Family Account and is manageable from the parent's consumer account.
Rollout state
All seven public kids-trial locations are disabled. Ridge Hill remains the authorized control, but it has no upcoming Program 61 occurrences in the current audit window and still needs controlled write acceptance. The six expansion sites remain API-authorization blocked.
HubSpot cutover
The app now skips the legacy form event by default, preventing the old 11-step account guide and legacy Deal workflows from also firing. Keep HUBSPOT_SUBMIT_LEGACY_TRIAL_FORM=false.
