Why this was proposed in May

The booking app has a graceful-degradation path: when MindBody is down, the app still submits to HubSpot as status: manual_review so a human can follow up. The May test recorded a reCAPTCHA-related rejection, but this archived page does not prove the current cause or scope.

Do not assume the toggle is the fix. Reproduce the current server-to-server request first and compare it with every browser-embedded form that would lose the visible challenge.

Historical portal steps — do not execute

  1. Log into HubSpot (app.hubspot.com) as an admin with Marketing permissions.
  2. Marketing → Forms.
  3. Open the Court 16 Trial form. Confirm the form ID in the URL matches 3e966ac4-872e-49ec-9b93-1f114fa6d39b.
  4. Settings tab (top of the form editor).
  5. Scroll to the spam-protection section.
  6. Uncheck "Protect this form from spam with Google reCAPTCHA."
  7. Publish / Update to save.
The form stays live through the change. Embeds on court16.com continue working; they simply stop requiring users to prove they're not robots. If spam becomes a problem in practice, HubSpot's built-in honeypot field and Akismet-style filtering kick in automatically — no action needed.

Current verification requirement

Do not test by submitting through a route that can create durable Mindbody records. First reproduce the HubSpot Forms API request in an isolated, non-customer fixture; inventory every live embed and nurture consumer; define spam metrics and rollback ownership; then request approval for a reversible form test. This archive is not the test plan.
