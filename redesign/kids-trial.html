The pathway

Red → Orange → Green → Yellow

We don't race kids through. Each ball color is a real milestone. Most kids spend 1–2 years at each level — that's how the best players are built.

Red Ball · ages 4–8

The introduction

36-foot court. Slow foam ball. Coordination & fundamentals. Pure fun.

Orange Ball · ages 9–10

Real rallies

60-foot court. Ball that travels but doesn't fly. Forehand & backhand structure.

Green Ball · ages 11–12

Strategy in

Full court. 75% compression. Players add spin, serves, and start point play.

Yellow Ball · ages 13+

The real deal

Standard tennis ball. Match play, league prep, tournament tracks for the committed.
